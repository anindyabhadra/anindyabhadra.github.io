I am a Professor in the <a href="https://www.stat.purdue.edu/">Department of Statistics</a> and a <a href="https://www.purdue.edu/academics/faculty-affairs/awards/faculty-scholars.html">University Faculty Scholar</a> at Purdue University. 

My recent research concerns probabilistic machine learning and uncertainty quantification methodology for graphical models with intractable normalizing constants, highly multivariate Gaussian processes, and generative modeling of manifold-valued data. I work on biological and environmental applications.

I currently serve as an Associate Editor for the _Annals of Applied Statistics_, the _Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics_, _Statistical Analysis and Data Mining_, and _Sankhya A_. Previously I served as an Associate Editor for the _Journal of the American Statistical Association (Theory &amp; Methods)_.
